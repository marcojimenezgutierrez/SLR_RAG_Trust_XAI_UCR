Title: Looking Through
Abstract: 3D Gaussian Splatting (GS) has shown impressive novel view synthesis results for static scenes. However, it is not straightforward to apply it to dynamic scenes, especially when tracking and mapping are required. In this paper, we propose GS-SLAM, a new system that combines GS with Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) for accurate 3D reconstruction of dynamic scenes. Our system initializes a GS representation from a set of keyframes and optimizes it jointly with camera poses and a coarse voxel map. We introduce a novel densification and pruning scheme that adapts the Gaussian representation to the scene content. Extensive experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that GS-SLAM outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of reconstruction quality and tracking accuracy.
